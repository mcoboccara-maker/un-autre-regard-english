Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

An Other Perspective (hereinafter "the App") is published by Marco Boccara. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Data Collected

The App may collect the following data:

  • Account data: email address, first name, date of birth (optional), when creating an account.
  • Usage data: thoughts, reflections, and emotions entered by the user within the App.
  • Preferences: spiritual, literary, psychological, and philosophical sources selected by the user.

2. Use of Data

The collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide personalized insights based on the sources chosen by the user.
  • Maintain the user's reflection history for personal use.
  • Improve the user experience of the App.

Your thoughts and reflections are sent to the Anthropic API (Claude) to generate insights. This data is processed in accordance with Anthropic's privacy policy.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored locally on your device and securely on our servers. It is never sold or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with any third party, except for:

  • Anthropic: for AI-powered insight generation (data is anonymized).
  • Firebase (Google): for authentication and secure storage.

5. Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your data, including encrypted communications (HTTPS) and secure storage.

6. User Rights

In accordance with GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access: obtain a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your data.
  • Portability: receive your data in a readable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at: appunautreregard@gmail.com

7. Guest Mode

The App offers a guest mode that does not require registration. Guest mode data is stored only on the device and is cleared at each new session.

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date.
