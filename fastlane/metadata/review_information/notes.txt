An Other Perspective is a CULTURAL READING AND REFLECTION app, NOT a therapy or medical app.

GUIDELINE 2.1 - INFORMATION NEEDED (answers):

1) Therapeutic vocabulary: the description has been updated to remove all therapeutic terms (ACT, transactional analysis, Gestalt, humanistic approach, systemic approach). These were only source labels, never a therapeutic offering. The app presents excerpts and reflections inspired by philosophical, literary and spiritual texts only.

2) AI-generated psychotherapy: NO. The app does not provide psychotherapy. The AI rephrases or extracts citations from public-domain or classical texts of philosophy, literature and spirituality. No diagnosis, no treatment plan, no therapeutic relationship.

3) Licensed professional: the app was developed by a single individual (Marco Boccara), without partnership with a licensed mental health professional. The disclaimer in the description makes this clear and directs users to qualified professionals for any psychological care.

GUIDELINE 5.1.1(v) - ACCOUNT DELETION (implemented in this build):
- Email/password account creation has been REMOVED from the app.
- Sign in with Google / Apple / Facebook remains available, and an in-app account deletion flow is now provided.
- WHERE: on the login screen, a "Delete my account" link is shown below the sign-in buttons.
- HOW TO TEST (important - the list only shows accounts that have signed in on this device):
  1) On the login screen, sign in once with Apple, Google or Facebook.
  2) Log out (logout icon at the top-right of the main menu) to return to the login screen.
  3) Tap "Delete my account": the account you just used is now listed.
  4) Tap "Delete" next to it, then confirm in the dialog.
- This permanently deletes the profile, saved values and reflections. All account data is stored locally on the device; there is no server-side account to keep.

TO TEST THE APP:
- On the login screen, tap "Enter as guest" to access all features without signing in.
- Type a thought, pick a source (philosopher, eastern wisdom, spiritual tradition), receive a reflection.
